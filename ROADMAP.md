# Roadmap

- **Hide the measured pitch.** A setting to turn off the thin line of the
  recording's measured pitch behind each contour.
- **Tone color palettes.** Choose among the common tone color schemes, or
  define your own, in place of the Pleco/MDBG colors.
- **Landscape layout.** Put the staves beside the answer buttons when the
  phone is turned, rather than one narrow column. A home-screen app is held in
  portrait; a Safari tab cannot be.
- **Voice range calibration.** A one-time step in which the listener says mā
  má mǎ mà, to set the range Say it draws on before enough takes have taught
  it.
- **Pitch-only playback.** Play a recording's measured pitch as a hummed tone,
  without the consonants and vowels, so only the tone shape is left to hear.
- **Minimal-pair drill.** For a confusion the listener keeps making (2-4 heard
  as 3-4), play two words that differ only there and ask which came first.
- **Contrast runs.** Before asking, play the same syllables in two tone
  patterns back to back (shíjiān, shìjiàn) as a short warm-up.
- **Batched downloads.** Fetch recordings a family at a time, as one file split
  back into saved recordings, so the first download makes ~200 requests
  instead of ~9,500.
- **Status and help.** Once the repository is public, a README section saying
  what the drill covers and where to report a problem.
